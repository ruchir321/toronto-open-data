# Climate Change Related Datasets

Here are the relevant datasets related to climate change published by the City of Toronto:

## 1. Current and Future Climate
**ID:** `current-and-future-climate`
**Description:** This dataset presents how Toronto's climate has changed in recent history and what future climate conditions could look like for the remainder of the 21st century under two potential greenhouse gas emissions scenarios. It includes climate projections for 53 variables.
**Resources:** CSV, JSON, XML.

## 2. TransformTO Community Feedback
**ID:** `transformto-community-feedback`
**Description:** Contains raw community feedback from TransformTO community engagement (2015-2016). Ideas on low-carbon initiatives to help Toronto achieve its 80% greenhouse gas emissions reduction goal by 2050.
**Resources:** XLSX.
